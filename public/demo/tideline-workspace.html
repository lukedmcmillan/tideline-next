MEPC 84: what the revised text changes

A concise internal briefing on the current negotiating text, the response timetable and the questions shipping teams need to settle before the session.

What changed

The levy remains in the consolidated draft circulated to member states. It had been absent from the previous two versions. The fuel standard also remains under discussion.

Why it matters

Compliance planning now needs to keep two possible measures in view. Fuel strategy, emissions data and contract assumptions may need to be tested against more than one outcome.

What needs to happen next

  • Confirm who owns the flag state response.
  • Compare the consolidated draft with the previous version.
  • Prepare a short decision note before the session opens on 26 July.
