4Standing rules for every session

  • Visual contract: the mockup is the acceptance test. If the live page and the mockup differ, the live page is wrong or the mockup gets consciously revised here first.
  • Plan-mode zones unchanged: stories, velocity_scores, divergences, document_queue, Stripe/Resend/auth, crons, migrations. All migrations in this plan are additive; anything else stops and asks.
  • Export-grade discipline: no UPDATE ever rewrites a historical score, classification, or divergence. Corrections are new rows.
  • Numbers are doors: a module that displays a count must link to its evidence, or it does not merge.
  • Honest empties: "backfill in progress", "calibrating", "quiet" render as designed. Nothing fakes data it does not have.
  • Old dashboard stays live until S6 passes its gate; build on parallel routes, switch atomically.
